Natasha

Her Story

From a young age, Natasha learned the importance of family, perseverance, and caring for others. She grew up in poverty, and because her parents worked long hours, she spent much of her childhood with other family members. Much of her time was spent with her grandparents and helping care for her great-grandparents. She believes these experiences helped shape her into the caring person she is today.

As she got older, she experienced challenges that changed the way she viewed herself and the world around her. Her first marriage ended abruptly, and she eventually learned that forgiveness was sometimes necessary even when it was not deserved. She considers this experience an important lesson in being selfless, especially for the sake of her oldest son.

Education also became an important part of Natasha's life. Coming from a family where no one had attended college, she came to understand how important education could be in breaking the cycle of poverty. She earned her GED while living in Alaska and raising two children, later continued her education, earned her LPN, and eventually decided to change career paths. Throughout this process, her husband has been an important source of encouragement. She credits him with believing in her and standing beside her even during times when she doubted herself.

Returning to school in her 30s while raising four children has been one of her greatest challenges. Learning how to manage school, family, and everything else required her to develop better time management and push herself when things became difficult. Completing her GED and continuing her education showed her that she was capable of accomplishing things she once doubted she could do.

Outside of school and her career goals, Natasha enjoys meal prepping and making healthier desserts for her family. She also enjoys exploring different cultures and places. Her time in Alaska helped her discover how much she enjoys exploring and learning about new places. In the future, she hopes to travel to places such as Italy, France, and Ireland.

Today, Natasha is continuing her education with the goal of becoming an intelligence analyst. She hopes to eventually work for the FBI while continuing to support her family and build a future where both she and her family can succeed. Her husband and children remain an important part of that future.
